# Code of Conduct

Nadi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is the part that applies day to day. The linked text is the full standard and governs where this summary is silent.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to the project or to Swift.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback on your work without getting defensive, and give it kindly.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a thread.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@theyudhiztira](https://github.com/theyudhiztira), is responsible for enforcement. To report a problem, use the "Report a vulnerability" button under the repository's Security tab, which sends a private report, and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, or block a person from the project, and will say why. Responses follow the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
